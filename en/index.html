HUNGARIAN COMMUNITY · MÁLAGA

Connect. Preserve. Build together.

A project to establish an independent Hungarian cultural and community association in Málaga. A place for people to meet, sustain the Hungarian language and foster Hungarian–Spanish dialogue.

Discover the initiative →

A place for a community to grow.

Our aim is to connect Hungarian people living in or connected to Málaga and the surrounding area. The association is still being established. This website presents the intended mission; it does not claim an officially registered status.

In preparationRegistration, leadership, contact details and the event calendar are pending publication. Online membership applications and payments are not available.

Culture and heritage

Language and generations

Ideas for Hungarian-language, family and children’s activities.

Community and connection

Opportunities to bring together Hungarians across Málaga and the Costa del Sol.

Hungarian–Spanish dialogue

Exploring partnerships with local cultural and civic initiatives.
