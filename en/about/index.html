HUNGARIAN COMMUNITY · MÁLAGA

Who are we?

An independent Hungarian community initiative for people living in or connected to the Costa del Sol. A meeting place for families, entrepreneurs, professionals and people interested in culture, built on human connection, professional cooperation and mutual support. The proposed association is not part of any federation or umbrella organisation.

Our mission

The Hungarian Community of Málaga is an initiative to establish an independent, non-profit association for Hungarians and people with Hungarian ties throughout the Costa del Sol. It is grounded in meaningful personal connections, shared values, Hungarian culture and Mediterranean life. Our ambition is an open, thoughtful and mutually supportive community bringing together families, entrepreneurs, executives, professionals and creatives.

Four pillars

Community and mutual support: trust, friendship and practical exchange. Business and professional connections: networking, mentoring and cooperation. Culture and inspiration: art, literature, music and Hungarian–Spanish dialogue. Families and generations: shared experiences, connections across generations and preservation of Hungarian language and traditions. These activities are planned, not yet operational.

Full organisational autonomy

The Hungarian Community of Málaga is intended to be an autonomous association. It is not an umbrella organisation and is not a member, chapter or subordinate unit of any federation or umbrella organisation. It will have its own membership, decision-making and programmes. It respects existing Hungarian organisations in the Costa del Sol and Spain, including the Madách Association. Any future cooperation would not imply institutional membership, representation or subordination.
