HUNGARIAN COMMUNITY · MÁLAGA

Who are we?

A new Hungarian community initiative in Málaga.

Our mission

The Hungarian Community of Málaga is an initiative to establish an independent, non-profit cultural and community association. It aims to connect Hungarians, families with Hungarian ties and people interested in Hungarian culture who live, work, study or spend extended periods in Málaga and the Costa del Sol. Our intention is to support Hungarian cultural identity alongside active participation in Andalusian society.

What we aim to do

We plan to nurture Hungarian language and heritage through family, children's and intergenerational gatherings; literary, musical, historical and artistic activities; community information exchange and volunteering. We also aim to encourage Hungarian–Spanish cultural dialogue and cooperation with local institutions and civil society. These are proposed activities, not existing services.

Independence and cooperation

This initiative is conceived as a local association with direct individual participation, not a federation. It does not represent other Hungarian associations in the Costa del Sol or Spain, coordinate their memberships or claim exclusive representation of the community. It respects the independence, programmes and community relationships of the Madách Association and other established organisations. Its purpose is not to create a competing national network or Hungarian school, but to develop complementary local opportunities based on actual needs. Any joint activity would require prior consultation and the agreement of those involved.

Formation and transparency

The official registered name, address, registration number, tax identification number, governing board, membership conditions and contact details are pending publication. Legal registration has not been verified. This website currently describes an initiative in preparation.

Our relationship with the Madách Association

The Madách Association has documented cultural and community activities across Spain, including the Costa del Sol. We respect this existing work. The proposed Hungarian Community of Málaga is intended as an independent, Málaga-centred local meeting point; it does not seek to take over Madách's membership, programmes, educational activities or representative role. Any cooperation would require prior consultation and mutual agreement.

Sources and clarification

Madách's presence in Spain and the Costa del Sol is documented by NYEOMSZSZ and the Kőrösi Csoma Sándor Programme's report from Estepona. Current local activities and the division of responsibilities still require consultation.
