Who are we?

A new Hungarian community initiative in Málaga.

The proposed association aims to establish a cultural and community meeting point in Málaga and its surroundings, for people who want to stay connected to Hungarian language, culture and one another.

This is an independent local initiative, not a federation. It does not automatically represent other organizations. Programmes and partnerships will begin once the required resources and agreements are in place.
